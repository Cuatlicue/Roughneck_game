# Roughneck_game
Roughneck: a magic-powered run-and-jump platformer by Citlalli Studios. A coatimundi, a quetzal and a bison team up with the Aztec gods to take down Baron Crude and CRUDE CO. Flashy magic powers, three stages, giant bosses and New Game+. Created by Alan Escalante.

Controls: Arrows/WASD move and aim, Z/Space/K jump (press jump again in the air for a high backflip jump), X/J cast your power, C god power, V swap god, P pause, M mute. Touch: pad moves, A jumps (tap A again in the air to backflip), B casts, GOD and SWAP use god powers. You can walk back through a level at any time.
